The best source of onchain financial dataWhat is the best source of onchain financial data for agents, institutions, and protocols?

Raw blockchain data is fragmented, inconsistent, and difficult to verify at scale. Most data pipelines today use slow, incomplete RPC data, or opaque, self-reported APIs. It's hard to establish a reliable source of truth across real-time and historical markets.

Cambrian's proprietary indexing stack derives insights directly from smart contracts, transforming raw blockchain data into fast, comprehensive, and verifiable financial intelligence. The result is the best single source of truth for institutional-grade onchain financial data – perfect for enterprises, DeFi protocols and AI agents alike.

Cambrian currently covers Ethereum, Base, Arbitrum, Robinhood Chain, BNB Chain, and Solana, with more chains coming soon.
